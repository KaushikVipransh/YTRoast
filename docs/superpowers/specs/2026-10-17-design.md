## Part 1 — Reference analysis

The reference is a photographer's landing page ("LET'S CAPTURE YOUR MOMENT") in a **retro pop-collage / zine** style. What makes it work:

### Colour
A small, warm, saturated palette on a paper-white base. Every colour is a flat fill. There are no gradients. Navy does all the outlining and text, which holds the busy collage together.

| Role | Approx. colour | Where it appears |
|---|---|---|
| Frame / primary | Sky blue `#5AA9D6` | outer frame, headline fill, arch behind portrait, stars |
| Warm band | Butter yellow `#F3D66B` | middle band, "CAPTURE" fill, hands |
| Hot accent | Tangerine `#EE9A5C` | bottom band, jacket, stars |
| Ink | Navy `#1F2A44` | every outline, all text, headline shadow |
| Paper | Off-white `#F6F4EE` | top band, crumpled paper base |
| Pops | Leaf green `#93C572`, brick red `#D9583B` | sparkles, camera body, sticker accents |

### Layout
- **Horizontal colour bands** (white → yellow → orange) with **torn / brushed edges**, stacked like layers of paper.
- A huge headline sits centre stage, with collage elements overlapping it: illustrations on the left, a cutout photo below, and a badge on the right.
- Small text blocks are pushed to the left and right edges, which leaves the centre to the visuals.
- **Faint graph-paper grids** sit in corners and behind the subject as texture.
- Things deliberately overlap and break out of their containers, like the hand cutout bleeding off the left edge and the stickers overlapping the photo.

### Typography
- **Display:** ultra-heavy, chunky, all caps. Fill colour + thick navy outline + a hard navy **offset shadow** (3D extrude), with a subtle grain/distress texture. Two fill colours alternate line by line.
- **Body / UI:** small **condensed sans-serif** in navy, tight line height, short lines.
- **Nav:** condensed caps, each item prefixed with a ✦ four-point star. The active item is circled with a **hand-drawn oval**.
- **Script:** a handwritten signature adds a personal touch.

### Component style
- **Stickers:** "LOL 😂" and "LMAO ✌️" as tilted text stickers with heavy outlines and a white die-cut border.
- **Cutouts:** a real photo cut out and set into a flat-colour arch, plus a **halftone grayscale** cutout with a white sticker border.
- **Flat cartoon illustrations** (camera, hands) with navy outlines and the same palette.
- **Rotating circular text badge** ("MAKE YOUR LIFE HAPPY · GO").
- **Four-point sparkle stars** scattered at different sizes and colours.
- **Textures:** crumpled paper, grain, and torn edges everywhere. Nothing looks digital-clean.
- Retro objects: a Polaroid camera and a **VHS tape** over the eyes. These are physical media nostalgia, which fits a "what you watched" product well.

**Mood:** loud, playful, handmade, nostalgic, a little chaotic but held together by the strict palette and navy ink.

## Part 2 — The concept: "The Evidence Zine"

We keep the reference's **collage-zine language** and give it a story that fits a roast:

> Your YouTube history has been seized. We made a zine out of the evidence.

The site is a **tabloid exposé of your watch habits**, put together by hand from scraps: video thumbnails cut out like evidence photos, taped to paper, circled in red marker, stamped **EXHIBIT A**, and annotated with savage handwritten notes. The reference's stickers become **reaction stickers** ("BRO.", "3AM???", "SEEN IT", "SKIP AD"). Its VHS tape becomes our **mascot object**: a tape labelled with your name.

What makes it ours and not a copy:
| Reference | Caught Watching |
|---|---|
| Polaroid camera illustration | **VHS tape + chunky CRT TV** illustrations (our mascots) |
| "LOL" / "LMAO" stickers | Roast stickers: "BRO.", "3AM???", "CAUGHT", "SEEN IT", "TOUCH GRASS" |
| Photo cutout in a colour arch | **Halftoned video thumbnails** cut out and taped on |
| Hand-drawn oval on nav item | **Red-marker circles and scribbles** marking the worst evidence |
| Rotating "MAKE YOUR LIFE HAPPY" badge | Rotating stamp: **"OFFICIALLY ROASTED · NO REFUNDS ·"** |
| Calm photographer bio text | **Case-file labels** in mono ("EXHIBIT C · 03:47 AM · TUE") |
| No red | **Marker red** added as the "roast" accent: the colour of shame |

## Part 3 — Design tokens

### Colour
```css
:root {
  /* paper & ink */
  --paper:        #F6F4EE;  /* main background */
  --paper-shade:  #E9E4D8;  /* folded/crumpled shadow tones */
  --ink:          #1F2A44;  /* text, outlines, shadows */
  --ink-soft:     #4A5470;  /* secondary text (AA on paper) */

  /* zine colours (from reference) */
  --sky:          #5AA9D6;
  --butter:       #F3D66B;
  --tangerine:    #EE9A5C;
  --leaf:         #93C572;
  --brick:        #D9583B;

  /* new: the roast accent */
  --marker:       #E2362B;  /* red marker circles, scribbles, stamps */

  /* functional */
  --outline-w:    3px;      /* standard ink outline */
  --shadow-hard:  5px 5px 0 var(--ink);
  --shadow-press: 2px 2px 0 var(--ink);
}
```
**Contrast rules:** body text is always `--ink` (or `--ink-soft`) on paper, butter, sky, or tangerine. All pass WCAG AA. **Never put white text on sky or butter.** `--marker` is for decoration and big display text only, never small text.

**Light only.** The paper metaphor doesn't survive dark mode, so v1 has no dark theme.

### Typography (all free, Google Fonts)
| Role | Font | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Display | **Bowlby One** | headlines, roast verdict, big stats. All caps. Outline + hard shadow |
| UI / labels | **Barlow Condensed** (600/800) | nav, buttons, section labels, sticker text |
| Body | **Barlow** (400/600) | roast lines, report captions, guides |
| Marker | **Permanent Marker** | handwritten annotations, scribbled reactions |
| Evidence | **Space Mono** | timestamps, counts, "EXHIBIT" labels, case IDs |

**Scale (mobile → desktop):**
- Display XL `clamp(48px, 13vw, 120px)` / line-height 0.9
- Display L `clamp(36px, 9vw, 72px)`
- Roast line `clamp(20px, 5vw, 28px)` Barlow 600 / line-height 1.25
- Body `17px` / 1.5
- Label `14px` Barlow Condensed 800, letter-spacing 0.06em, caps
- Mono `13px`

**The display treatment (signature look):**
```css
.display {
  font-family: "Bowlby One";
  color: var(--sky);
  -webkit-text-stroke: var(--outline-w) var(--ink);
  paint-order: stroke fill;
  text-shadow: 4px 4px 0 var(--ink);      /* hard extrude */
  text-transform: uppercase;
}
.display .alt { color: var(--butter); }    /* alternate lines, like "CAPTURE" */
.display .hot { color: var(--marker); }    /* the roast word: "CAUGHT", "ROASTED" */
```
Add a grain overlay (SVG noise, `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, ~15% opacity) over display text for the distressed print feel.

### Texture & shape kit
- **Crumpled paper:** one optimised WebP/AVIF texture tiled on `body`, ~40 KB.
- **Torn edges:** 4–5 SVG `clip-path` masks for band edges, picked at random per section so no two edges match.
- **Graph-paper grid:** CSS `background-image` linear-gradient lines, `--ink` at 8% opacity, 24px squares, used as corner patches.
- **Sparkle star:** one SVG four-point star, coloured via `currentColor`. Scattered 3–6 per screen at 12–48px, with a slight random rotation.
- **Tape strips:** semi-transparent butter-coloured rectangles, rotated ±8°, at the corners of thumbnail cutouts.
- **Halftone filter for thumbnails:** `filter: grayscale(1) contrast(1.4)` + an SVG dot-pattern overlay with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`. Thumbnails load from YouTube's thumbnail CDN.
- **Marker strokes:** a set of hand-drawn SVG paths (circle, underline, arrow, scribble-out) in `--marker`, animated with `stroke-dashoffset` to look drawn live.

## Part 4 — Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("ROAST ME")**: butter fill, 3px ink outline, `--shadow-hard`, Barlow Condensed 800 caps, radius 14px. Press state: translate(3px,3px) + `--shadow-press` (it physically "presses" in). Min height 56px.
- **Secondary**: paper fill, same outline and shadow.
- **Google sign-in**: must follow Google's branding guidelines (official logo, "Sign in with Google" text). Wrap it in our outline + shadow frame, but don't restyle the button itself.
- **Danger (Delete)**: paper fill, `--marker` text and outline.

### Sticker
Text sticker with white die-cut border: `--ink` outline → 4px white stroke → drop shadow `0 3px 0 rgba(31,42,68,.25)`. Random rotation −12°…+12°. Fills rotate through sky / butter / tangerine / leaf. Optional emoji. When it appears it pops in: scale 0 → 1.15 → 1, with a spring.

Roast sticker set: `BRO.` · `3AM???` · `CAUGHT` · `SEEN IT` · `SKIP AD` · `TOUCH GRASS` · `NO WAY` · `💀` · `EXHIBIT A`

### Evidence card (core unit of the report)
```
┌──────────────────────────────┐  ← paper or butter fill, 3px ink outline,
│ EXHIBIT C · 03:47 · TUE      │    hard shadow, tilted ±1.5°
│ ┌────────────┐               │  ← mono label
│ │ halftoned  │▓ tape         │
│ │ thumbnail  │               │  ← thumbnail cutout, tape on corners
│ └────────────┘               │
│  "you rewatched this 31×"    │  ← Barlow 600
│        ⟵ (red marker circle around "31×")
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

### Stat block
A huge number in display style (sky fill, ink outline, extrude). The label underneath is in Barlow Condensed caps, and a marker annotation arrow points at the number with a handwritten aside ("that's 9 full days, btw").

### Roast line
Each line on its own strip of paper ("ransom note" strip): paper or butter fill, torn left/right edges, slight tilt alternating ±1°, Barlow 600. The **evidence word** (channel, title, number) is wrapped in a red marker circle or underline.

### Rotating stamp
Circular SVG text "OFFICIALLY ROASTED · NO REFUNDS · " rotating slowly (30s per turn), with the centre showing a 🔥 or "100%". Placed overlapping the roast headline. Directly inspired by the reference badge.

### Nav
Small and condensed like the reference: `✦ HOW IT WORKS  ✦ PRIVACY  ✦ ROAST ME`. The current page is circled with a hand-drawn oval in `--marker`.

### Progress / loading
A **VHS tape** illustration with spinning reels, and a counter that "rewinds" through the user's real channel names while it works. The status line in mono changes every ~1.5s: `reading 48,213 videos…` → `found the 3am stuff…` → `consulting your therapist…` → `writing the roast…`

Design mobile-first (375px). Desktop centres a 480–560px column for the app flow and goes wide only on the landing page.

```
│   HISTORY IS          [LOL] │  display, sky
│   EVIDENCE.  ✦              │  display, marker red ("EVIDENCE.")
│~~~~~~~~torn edge~~~~~~~~~~~~│
│  (yellow band)              │
│   [CRT TV illustration with │
│    halftone thumbnails      │  collage cluster; stickers "3AM???" "BRO."
│    spilling out]   (stamp)  │  rotating stamp overlaps
│                             │
│ we read what you watched.   │  Barlow, ink
│ then we roast you for it.   │
│                             │
│ [ ROAST ME — QUICK  ⚡ ]    │  primary: Google sign-in path

│    leaves your phone        │  summary + sample titles DO go to the AI)
│~~~~~~~~torn edge~~~~~~~~~~~~│
│  (tangerine band)           │

│  COULD BE YOU")             │
└─────────────────────────────┘
```
On desktop, the headline sits centre, the collage overlaps it like in the reference, and small text blocks pin to the left and right edges.

### 5.2 Choose your poison (path picker)
Two big **tickets** side by side (stacked on mobile):
- **QUICK ROAST** · butter ticket · "30 seconds · from your likes & subs · mild-ish"
- **FULL ROAST** · marker-red outline ticket · "5–10 min setup · your actual watch history · no survivors"

### 5.3 Takeout guide
A step-by-step **zine page sequence**, one step per screen, swipeable. Each step has an annotated screenshot of Google Takeout with red marker circles on what to tap:
1. Open Takeout (deep link) → "Deselect all"
2. Tick **YouTube and YouTube Music**
3. "All YouTube data included" → keep only **history**
4. Formats → history → **JSON** (HTML works too, JSON is faster)
5. Export once → wait for Google's email (can take a while: "go touch grass, we'll be here")
6. Come back, **drop the zip**

The drop zone is a big dashed-outline "evidence bag" with a VHS tape illustration: "DROP THE EVIDENCE HERE". On drag-over it wiggles and turns butter.

### 5.4 Processing → roasting
Full screen, sky band background, VHS loader (Part 4). For the full path, real numbers appear as they're counted: `48,213 videos · 1,902 channels · 312 nights after 2am`. These are real, which builds dread.

### 5.5 Roast reveal (the headline moment)
```
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│ CASE #A7F3K2      (stamp)   │
│                             │
│  THE VERDICT:               │  label
│  "A MAN WITH 4 SELF-HELP    │  display L, sky/butter alternating,
│   PLAYLISTS AND ZERO SELF"  │  stamp overlapping corner
│~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~│
│ ▭ you watched ⭕"how to wake │  roast lines on ransom strips,
│   up at 5am"⭕ at 4:12am.   │  revealed one at a time on tap/scroll
│   twice.             [BRO.] │  with a sticker slapped on some
│ ▭ 31 rewatches of the same  │
│   minecraft ending. it's    │
│   not getting better.       │
│ ▭ …                         │
│                             │
│ did this hit?  👍  👎        │
│ [ SHARE THE DAMAGE ]  [ ⬇ ] │  primary share + download story
│ ▼ SCROLL FOR YOUR FULL FILE │  marker arrow, bouncing
└─────────────────────────────┘
```
Lines drop in one by one (tap or scroll to advance, ~600ms stagger), and marker circles draw themselves on the evidence word. The private state shows a small mono banner: `🔒 only you can see this. share when ready.`

### 5.6 The Report ("your file")
A vertical scroll of full-width **zine spreads**, each on its own colour band with torn edges, alternating paper → butter → sky → tangerine. Each spread has one big idea:

1. **The Numbers.** Total videos, est. hours ("est." in marker), channels. Giant stat blocks.
2. **Top 5 Channels.** Ranked evidence cards with channel name, count, and a one-line caption. #1 gets a marker crown scribble.
3. **Most Rewatched.** Halftone thumbnail cutouts taped up, with rewatch counts circled.
4. **The Clock.** A 24-hour radial "clock face" chart hand-drawn in ink, with bars in tangerine and the 12am–5am wedge hatched in marker red. Big stat: "% after midnight".
5. **Your Eras.** A timeline of torn paper strips, each era named by the AI ("the gym-tok era · mar–jun 2025"), with a top thumbnail per era.
6. **Longest Binge.** A film-strip of thumbnails from that day + "11 hours. on a tuesday."
7. **What You Searched** (if search history). Ransom-note style search queries.
8. **Contradictions.** A side-by-side "what you watch vs. who you think you are", split down the middle with a torn edge.
9. **Your Personality.** A giant sticker-badge personality label ("THE 3AM PHILOSOPHER") + 2–3 sentence description. This is the screenshot moment.
10. **Outro.** The rotating stamp, share buttons, "roast a friend → send them this link".

Quick-path reports use only the spreads with data (1, 2, 8, 9, plus "Subscriptions You Abandoned" and "Playlists Exposed") and end with the upsell spread: **"THAT WAS THE WARM-UP."** → full roast CTA.

Charts are hand-made SVG in the house style (ink outlines, flat fills, slight wobble), never default chart-library styling.

### 5.7 Public share page (`/r/[id]`, viewer is a friend)
Same roast + report, plus:
- A top strip with sticker: `👀 YOU'RE READING SOMEONE'S FILE`
- A sticky bottom CTA bar (butter, ink outline): **ROAST ME NEXT →**
- No owner controls.

### 5.8 Owner controls
A small paper tab pinned to the corner of the roast with the options: `SHARE / MAKE PRIVATE · COPY LINK · DOWNLOAD STORY · DELETE`. Delete opens a confirm dialog: "burn the evidence?" → [BURN IT] (marker red) / [keep]. The burn animation scribbles the roast out in red marker, then the paper crumples.

### 5.9 Deleted / not found / private
A crumpled paper ball illustration. "this file was burned." / "nothing here. or someone's hiding something." → ROAST ME button.

### 5.10 Oven full (quota hit)
A CRT TV showing static and the sticker "WE'RE AT CAPACITY". "too many people getting roasted right now. here's a quick one from your stats, come back later for the real thing." The template roast renders below with a small `TEMPLATE ROAST` stamp.

### 5.11 Errors
Specific and funny, always with a fix:
- Wrong file: "that's not your watch history, that's ___. we need `watch-history.json`." + link back to guide step 4.
- Empty history: "your history is empty. either you're a saint or you turned history off. both are suspicious."
- Google access denied: "you blinked. we need read-only YouTube access to roast you." [TRY AGAIN]

## Part 7 — Motion

| Moment | Motion |
|---|---|
| Stickers appear | spring pop: scale 0 → 1.15 → 1, rotate from 0 to final tilt |
| Bands enter on scroll | slide up 24px + fade, torn edge slightly "flaps" |
| Marker circles/underlines | `stroke-dashoffset` draw-on, 400–700ms, ease-out |
| Roast lines | stagger in 600ms apart. Each lands with a 2° wobble |
| Buttons | press into shadow (translate 3px). Hover lifts 1px |
| Stamp | slow 30s rotation |
| VHS loader | reels spin. Counter ticks |

**`prefers-reduced-motion`:** all motion becomes instant fades. The stamp stops rotating and roast lines appear all at once.

## Part 8 — Accessibility & practical rules
- All body text is ink on light fills (AA). Display text with outline counts as large text.
- Decorative stickers, stars, tape and textures are `aria-hidden`. Roast lines are real text, never images.
- Tilt never exceeds ±3° on anything people need to read. Stickers can tilt more because they're decoration.
- Tap targets ≥ 48px. The sticky CTA doesn't cover content (bottom padding reserved).
- Performance budget: textures ≤ 120 KB total, fonts subset to Latin, illustrations as inline SVG. Thumbnails lazy-load.
- Respect YouTube's branding guidelines: thumbnails link back to the video on YouTube, and the YouTube logo is not used as our brand mark.
