# Caught Watching — Product Requirements Document

*Working title: "Caught Watching". The name is a placeholder (see Open items). Avoid putting "YouTube" in the product name: YouTube's API branding rules forbid names that imply YouTube endorses the product.*

Date: 2026-10-17 · Status: Draft for approval

---

## 1. What this is

Caught Watching is a website. Anyone on the internet can sign in with Google or upload their YouTube watch history and get two things:

1. **The Roast.** A raw, savage, specific roast of their YouTube habits, written like a ruthless friend who has seen their history. r/RoastMe energy: short brutal one-liners, each one built on a real detail from their data.
2. **The Report.** A Wrapped-style breakdown of their watching: top channels, their "eras", 3am habits, binge streaks, guilty pleasures, a personality label. It's fun to scroll, full of real insights, and made to screenshot.

Each roast gets a **shareable link**. Friends open it, laugh, and want their own. That loop is how the product grows.

### Why it works
- People love being seen, even when it's brutal (see Spotify Wrapped, r/RoastMe, "AI roast my Instagram" trends).
- YouTube history is unusually revealing and personal. It's what you actually watch, not what you post.
- Being roasted is low-stakes. Sharing is the punchline, and every shared link is free distribution.

---

## 2. Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

### Goals
- **G1.** Deliver a roast that feels personal, specific, and human-written, so the user laughs out loud or winces.
- **G2.** Deliver a report that is genuinely insightful and fun to scroll, on par with Spotify Wrapped.
- **G3.** Make sharing effortless: one shareable link with a good-looking preview in WhatsApp, X, Instagram DMs and Discord, plus a downloadable story-sized image.
- **G4.** Support **real watch history** (Google Takeout upload) as the flagship path, and **one-click Google sign-in** (likes, subscriptions, playlists) as the quick path. Both ship at launch.
- **G5.** Run at **$0/month** on free tiers.

### Non-goals (not in v1)
- User accounts, login history, or "roast me again next year" timelines.
- Other platforms (Spotify, Netflix, Instagram).
- Languages other than English.
- Native mobile apps. (The site must be excellent on mobile web.)
- Monetisation of any kind.
- Roasting a friend by their username. You can only roast data you have access to (your own Google account, or a Takeout file you hold).

### Anti-goals (failure even if it technically works)
- **It reads like AI.** Phrases like "Ah, I see you're a fan of…", "Let's dive in", neat three-part lists, a closing compliment, generic jokes that could apply to anyone.
- **The roast is soft.** Topics are not avoided or toned down. Health, breakups, politics, sexuality, embarrassing searches: if it's in the data, it's fair game. *(Owner's explicit decision.)*
- **It costs money.** Any recurring bill is a failure of the plan.
- **Raw watch history is stored on our servers.** The Takeout file never leaves the user's browser.

## 3. Users

**Primary user:** a random person on the internet, most likely 16–30 *(guess)*, who arrived from a friend's shared roast link or a social post. They're on their **phone** *(guess, but likely: shared links get opened from chat apps)*. They want to be entertained in under 2 minutes and to send the result to the group chat.

**Secondary user:** the friend who opens a shared link. They don't sign in. They read someone else's roast, laugh, and hit "Roast me next".

## 4. User stories

### Arriving
- **US-1.** As a visitor from a shared link, I see my friend's roast and report straight away, with no login wall, and a clear "Roast me next" button.
- **US-2.** As a new visitor, I understand within 5 seconds what the site does, and that it's going to be brutal.

### Getting roasted: quick path
- **US-3.** As a user, I can tap "Sign in with Google", grant read-only YouTube access, and get roasted from my liked videos, subscriptions and playlists without doing anything else.
- **US-4.** As a user with almost no likes or subscriptions, I still get roasted, and the thin data itself is the joke ("3 liked videos in 6 years. who hurt you").
- **US-5.** As a quick-path user, I'm offered the full roast afterwards ("that was the warm-up. want the real damage?").

### Getting roasted: full path (Takeout)
- **US-6.** As a user, I get simple, illustrated step-by-step instructions for exporting my YouTube history from Google Takeout, including which boxes to tick (YouTube → history, JSON format).
- **US-7.** As a user, I know Takeout can take minutes to hours, and I know I can come back later. Google emails me when my export is ready.
- **US-8.** As a user, I can drag in the whole Takeout `.zip` (or just `watch-history.json` / `.html`). The site finds the right files itself.
- **US-9.** As a user, I'm clearly told my file is processed **on my device** and only a small summary is sent off to write the roast.
- **US-10.** As a user with years of history (100k+ entries), the upload processes without freezing my phone.

### Reading
- **US-11.** As a user, I watch a short "roasting…" sequence (≤ 20 s) that builds anticipation, then the roast is revealed.
- **US-12.** As a user, I can scroll from the roast into the full report.
- **US-13.** As a user, every joke and stat refers to real things from my data: real channel names, real video titles, real times.

### Sharing and control
- **US-14.** As a user, I see my roast privately first, then choose **Share** to make the link public.
- **US-15.** As a user, I can copy the link, use the phone's native share sheet, or download a story-sized image (1080×1920).
- **US-16.** As someone receiving the link, I see a rich preview card in the chat app (title + roast headline image).
- **US-17.** As the creator, I can delete my roast at any time, and the link then shows "this roast has been deleted".
- **US-18.** As the creator, I can make a public roast private again.

### When things go wrong
- **US-19.** If the site is at its free AI limit, I still get a roast (template-written from my stats), clearly but playfully labelled, with an option to come back for the full one.
- **US-20.** If my file is the wrong one, corrupted, or empty, I get a funny, specific error that tells me exactly what to do.

### 5.2 Stats engine (code, not AI)
Deterministic, tested code turns raw data into a **Summary**. The AI never does maths. Stats include:

| Stat | Quick path | Full path |
|---|---|---|
| Top channels (count) | from likes/subs | from watches |
| Top videos rewatched | — | ✓ |
| Total videos / estimated hours | likes count | ✓ (hours via enriched sample, labelled estimate) |
| Time-of-day profile, % after midnight, latest-night streak | — | ✓ |
| Day-of-week profile | — | ✓ |
| Longest binge (most videos in one sitting / one day) | — | ✓ |
| "Eras": dominant topic per quarter/month over time | — | ✓ |
| Topic mix (from categories + title keywords) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Contradictions (e.g., productivity content + 4am watching) | ✓ | ✓ |
| Searches: most repeated, weirdest, latest-night | — | ✓ (if F3) |
| Oldest subscription / subscriptions never watched | ✓ | ✓ |
| Thin-data flag (very few likes/subs) | ✓ | — |

Plus a **sample of real titles and channel names** chosen to give the AI good material: most-watched, most-repeated, latest-night, and odd outliers.

## 6. Success metrics

Targets are first guesses for a hobby launch and should be revised after the first week.

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Share rate** | % of completed roasts made public | ≥ 35% |
| **Viral loop** | new roasts started from a shared-link visit ÷ shared-link visits | ≥ 15% |
| **Takeout completion** | Takeout uploads ÷ users who opened the Takeout guide | ≥ 25% |
| **Quick → full upgrade** | quick-path users who later complete a Takeout roast | ≥ 10% |
| **Roast success** | roasts written by AI (not template fallback) ÷ all roasts | ≥ 95% |
| **Time to roast** | quick path: tap sign-in → roast visible (p75) | ≤ 25 s |
| **Delete rate** | roasts deleted ÷ roasts shared (high = it felt too exposing or bad) | watch; investigate if > 20% |
| **"Felt human"** | optional 👍/👎 "did this hit?" on the roast | ≥ 80% 👍 |
| **Monthly cost** | total spend | **$0** |

## 7. Voice spec (roast and report)

**Reference voice:** r/RoastMe. Short, brutal, one detail per line, no warm-up, no apology, no closing compliment.

> "4,000 hours of Andrew Huberman and you still go to bed at 4am. you're not optimizing, you're collecting."

**Rules**
- Every line references a **specific** piece of evidence (title, channel, count, timestamp).
- Short. Most lines ≤ 25 words.
- Lowercase internet voice is allowed. Swearing is allowed.
- Nothing is off-limits (owner's decision).
- Report copy is playful and sharp but **informative**: the joke sits next to a real insight, not instead of one.

**Banned (AI tells):** "Ah,", "Let's dive in", "It seems like", "Not only… but also", "a testament to", "journey", "delve", "tapestry", "Whether you're…", em dashes in roast lines, ending on encouragement, "but hey, at least…", three-item rhythm in every sentence, emoji spam.

## 8. Key risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Free AI softens or refuses harsh roasts | Breaks G1 | **Spike first**: test brutal prompts on Gemini and Groq before building. Pick the provider that holds up. Backup provider + template fallback |
| Google OAuth verification for the YouTube read scope | Until verified: max 100 test users + scary "unverified app" screen | Apply for verification early (free for this scope). Requires privacy policy, homepage, possibly a custom domain (~$10/yr, the only potential cost). The Takeout path needs no verification, so launch can lead with it |
| Takeout drop-off (export takes time) | Low full-path completion | Excellent guide. Quick roast first so they get value immediately and come back |
| Free-tier quotas hit on a viral day | Degraded roasts | Rate limits, template fallback, playful "oven is full" state |
| Public links expose very personal content | Embarrassment, deletions | Private by default, explicit Share step, easy unshare/delete |
| Forged summaries posted to our API | Abuse of free AI quota | Rate limits, strict schema + size limits on input |
| Takeout format changes | Parser breaks | Parse both JSON and HTML defensively. Fixture tests from real exports |

## 9. Decisions made by the planner (check these)
- **Re-roasting:** a user can generate a new roast any time within the rate limit. Each roast is a new link; old ones stay until deleted.
- **Quick → full upgrade** creates a new, separate roast. It doesn't overwrite the quick one.
- **Privacy wording** must never claim "nothing leaves your device". Accurate version: "your full history stays on your device; a summary with stats and some video titles is sent to write your roast."
- **No accounts.** Ownership is by a secret token in the creator's browser.
- **Light theme only** (see design doc).
- **No custom domain for now (owner's decision, 2026-10-17).** Launch on the free `*.vercel.app` subdomain. The Takeout path is open to everyone. The quick (Google sign-in) path runs in Google's testing mode, limited to 100 test users, until Google verification and the domain question are revisited.
